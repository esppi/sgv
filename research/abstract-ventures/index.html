05 · How the great investors would read it

Abstract is effectively a concentrated access fund with a seed program attached

Thiel · power law

One position, xAI, is modeled at about 1.95x Fund III. Cognition and Polymarket add about 0.6x more. Fund III is a power-law fund that depends on very few names. The question for diligence is whether that can repeat, or whether it was a single access event.

Druckenmiller · sizing

Fifteen percent of a seed fund in one formation round is hedge-fund sizing: be right, then be big. Most seed managers cap any one position at 3–5%. Naimi's edge looks like a willingness to concentrate when he has access, more than picking at seed.

Buffett · cash, not marks

Through 2026 the returns are mostly unrealized. SpaceX listed on June 12, 2026, so a standard 180-day lock-up ends around December 9. That makes early 2027 the moment Fund III can turn paper TVPI into real DPI. Ask whether they distribute in kind or sell.

Valentine · the next fund's math

For 3x gross, a $500M Fund IV must return $1.5B. At a modeled ~8% entry ownership, diluted to ~4% at exit, it needs about $37B of combined exit value. Fund IV is buying that with $11–12M led seeds into AI hardware, a different bet from Fund III.

Munger · invert

What breaks it: the access deals (xAI, SpaceX) are priced off one founder's story, and the 2021 vintage (Fund II) is modeled near benchmark. Remove xAI and Fund III drops to about 1.6x, which is good but not elite.

For SGV

Two lessons transfer to a fund of our size. Leading seeds (51% by Crunchbase) built the brand that won the allocation. Then sizing up hard in one rare access round made the fund. Plan reserves for one 10–15% concentrated position per fund, decided in advance.
